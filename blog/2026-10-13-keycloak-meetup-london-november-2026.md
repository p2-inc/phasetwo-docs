## What's actually planned

Short talks, workshops on Keycloak topics, and an open discussion. **The speaker list is not published yet**. If you have a topic you'd like to hear more about, or have a talk ready to go, reach out to us. The format is deliberately loose: this is the first one, and the point of a first meetup is to find out what the people in the room actually want from it.

What that means in practice is that the open discussion is likely to be the most useful hour of the afternoon, and it is worth turning up with a specific question rather than a general interest. The things people tend to want to compare notes on:

- **Upgrades.** Keycloak supports the latest release only, and the two previous even-numbered branches get security backports with [no release and no image built from them](/blog/keycloak-lts-backport-images). Everyone running Keycloak in production has an opinion about this, and most of them are frustrated.
- **What you had to find out the hard way** — the proxy header that broke your login flow, the realm export that didn't round-trip, the cache setting nobody documents. Our [production checklist](/blog/keycloak-production-checklist) came out of exactly these conversations.
- **Extensions and SPIs.** What people have built, and what they gave up on. Our [extensions are open source](/docs/introduction/open-source/) if you want to look at working examples before you come.

## Who it's for

Anyone who runs Keycloak or is thinking about it. Genuinely both ends — if you are evaluating it and have never deployed one, the half of the room that has been running it for five years is the most valuable thing in the building. If you are that half of the room, come and argue with the other veterans.

You do not need to be a Phase Two customer, and nobody will treat you like a lead. We are sponsoring the venue and the first two drinks at Nine Lives; that is the extent of the commercial content.
