:::tip
It is bad practice to use your admin user to sign in to an application.
:::

The local Keycloak from the examples repo already has a non-admin user, `demo` / `demo`. On any other Keycloak, add one:

1.  Open the Keycloak admin console and select your realm.
1.  Click **Users** in the menu.
1.  Click **Add user**.
1.  Fill out the **Username**, **Email**, **First name** and **Last name**. Click **Create**.
1.  Open the **Credentials** tab and click **Set password**. Enter a password for the user. For this tutorial, you can turn **Temporary** off.
1.  Click **Save**, then confirm with **Save password**.
