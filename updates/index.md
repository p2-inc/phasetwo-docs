# Monthly updates

Once a month we write up what actually changed: the Phase Two extensions that shipped a new
version, the official Keycloak releases and the CVEs they fixed, and the guides and posts we
published. It goes out by email on the first of the month and is archived here.

Nothing in here is a launch announcement. It is the list a person running Keycloak in
production would otherwise have to assemble by watching four GitHub repositories and an
advisories feed.

## Why this exists separately from the blog

The [blog](/blog/) is where we explain one thing properly. This is a digest: short entries
that point at the thing itself. Keeping them apart means the blog stays readable and the
digest stays skimmable, and neither has to pretend to be the other.

For the release-by-release detail on official Keycloak — what is in it, whether a CVE
applies to you, and whether the upgrade is urgent — we publish a dedicated article for
every upstream release. Those are on the blog, tagged
[release](/blog/tags/release/).
